TITLE
VueConfort

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Personalise reading comfort, with or without a vision prescription.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Adjust your display preferences at your own pace.

VueConfort brings your reading preferences together in a personal profile. Try it immediately, enter a correction you already know or import a vision prescription. No prescription or account is required.

YOUR VISUAL PROFILE

• Guided setup with or without a known correction
• Local import of a prescription photo or PDF, with text recognition on your phone
• Review and confirm each eye’s values before saving
• Calibration and personal settings you can refine at any time

REFINE THE PREVIEW

Adjust size, sharpness, contrast, soft light, text weight and intensity. Compare different scenes with the Original image, then save your preferences. These effects appear in the VueConfort preview; they do not mean that every other app is being transformed.

THE AIDS AVAILABLE ON YOUR PHONE

• Android magnification and floating controls after you voluntarily enable the accessibility service
• Local accessible-text reader, only when you choose Read
• Reading profiles and local rules for your needs
• Guidance to available Android and Samsung settings, including Relumino outlines on supported devices
• Status checks where supported, and restoration of settings changed automatically

Protected Samsung features remain under your control in the phone settings. VueConfort distinguishes available aids, those requiring your action and those not supported by your device.

YOUR DATA STAYS LOCAL

No account, advertising or network transfer by the app. Confirmed prescription values, preferences and calibrations stay in private app storage. The selected image/PDF and recognised text are used for the import and then cleaned up; the original document remains under your control. You can delete your prescription and profile.

The accessibility service displays controls, operates magnification, identifies the active app for your rules and reads accessible text only on request. The commercial version does not capture other apps’ screens or transmit their content.

CLEAR LIMITS

VueConfort provides comfort aids; results vary by person and device. It does not replace glasses or an eye examination, diagnose a condition or issue a prescription. Importing a prescription does not guarantee optical correction across the whole screen.

Some apps and protected windows expose no accessible text. VueConfort respects these limits. Android 9 or later; specific features vary by phone and Android version.

WHAT’S NEW — VERSION 1.1.0

Guided setup with or without a prescription, a central personal profile, local photo/PDF import with confirmation, a real-time visual equalizer and guided Android/Samsung aids. Application status and restoration are checked according to phone capabilities.
