# Privacy Policy — VueConfort

Last updated: August 4, 2026

VueConfort is an Android reading-comfort assistance app. It creates no account and adds no server, advertising, analytics or network transfer.

## Data processed

The app may process profiles and settings, calibrations, visual screening results and history, automation rules and, only when requested by the user, accessibility text exposed by the app currently displayed.

## Storage and transmission

Settings and results are kept locally in the app’s private storage. Accessibility text requested for the reader is used temporarily and locally; it is neither stored in history nor transmitted. The production version of VueConfort does not capture the screen or record passwords. Android app backup is disabled.

## Accessibility service

The service displays assistance controls and the VueConfort Magnifier based on native Android magnification, detects the active app for profile rules and extracts accessibility text on request for the reader. Some apps, images, videos, games, web views or protected windows may expose no text. VueConfort does not bypass these protections.

## Visual screening

The tests are on-screen screening tools: they do not provide a diagnosis and are not a prescription. They do not replace glasses, an optician or an ophthalmologist.

## User choices

In Settings, users can delete history, reset profiles, clear automation rules or all settings. They can also disable the accessibility service or uninstall the app. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s private local data.
