# Privacy Policy — VueConfort

Last updated: September 25, 2026

VueConfort is an Android reading-comfort assistance app. It creates no account and adds no server, advertising, analytics or network transfer.

## Data processed

The app locally processes display profiles and preferences, calibrations, visual exercise results and history, and user-selected automation rules. My display also stores requested settings, confirmations, available phone capabilities and previous values needed for restoration. The phone model and software version help identify supported features.

If you add a vision prescription, the values you enter or confirm, associated information and their history are stored locally. You may select an image or a PDF page: text recognition runs on the phone. Proposed values must be checked and confirmed before saving. The original document and recognised text are not retained in the saved prescription. A temporary private copy may be used during reading and is then deleted. An abrupt interruption can delay cleanup until the next startup or import.

## Storage and transmission

Saved data stays in the app’s private storage. VueConfort does not transmit it to a server. Android app backup is disabled. The commercial app and its Preview do not capture other apps’ screens to apply native display aids.

## Accessibility service and reader

After you voluntarily enable it, the service displays magnifier controls, controls native Android magnification and identifies the active app for your profile rules. When you choose **Read**, it processes accessible text and descriptions exposed by the current window and displays them in a local reader. This text stays in the service’s memory; it is not saved in history or transmitted. Some apps or protected windows expose no text; VueConfort does not bypass this restriction.

My display sends parameters to authorised phone features or guides you through Android/Samsung settings. It does not continuously analyse your screen’s content.

## Prescription and visual exercises

The aids, exercises and suggested settings do not provide a diagnosis or prescription and do not demonstrate optical correction of your eyesight. They do not replace glasses or an eye examination. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

## Your choices

You can delete your prescription and its history, delete your personal profile, clear histories or rules, and disable the accessibility service. Deleting a prescription does not delete the original document in Android.

For automatically changed settings, My display lets you keep the current values or restore the values recorded before the commands. A change made elsewhere is preserved. Restoration data stays local until restoration completes or you choose to keep the current settings. Samsung settings you change manually remain under your control.

Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes its private storage, but does not necessarily reset Android/Samsung display settings. Use restoration before deleting the app’s data if you want to return to the previous values.
